Welcome to Jamia

A food mart with the soul of a coffee lounge

Whether it is a quiet morning coffee, a family lunch, a work meeting or a late-night craving, Jamia gives you a warm table and a menu that has something for everyone.

Our kitchen cooks fragrant biryani and curries, flame-grilled meats, hearty pasta, stone-baked pizza and crisp burgers. Our lounge pours tea, coffee, bubble tea and cool mocktails all day long.

Come for a slow breakfast, a working lunch, an afternoon slice of cake or a late dinner with friends. With free Wi-Fi, parking and WhatsApp delivery, Jamia fits easily into your day.

Our story

Born from a love of good food and good company

Jamia grew from a simple idea: one welcoming place in Nakuru where every meal, from a quick coffee to a family feast, feels like an occasion.

Step inside and you will find bright interiors, friendly faces and a kitchen that treats every plate with care.

Today Jamia is a food mart, coffee lounge and restaurant in one, serving guests from morning until 11PM at Oginga Odinga Avenue in Nakuru.

How ordering works

From menu to table in three taps

No app and no sign-up. Your order goes straight to our WhatsApp, where we confirm it with you personally.

  1. Build your order

    Browse the menu, filter by category and add your favourites.

  2. Send it on WhatsApp

    Add your details and address. You see your delivery fee first, and we confirm food prices and your total on WhatsApp.

  3. Enjoy it hot

    We confirm on WhatsApp and bring it to your door or have it ready at the counter.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day, including weekends, from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Add dishes to your order, choose your delivery area and send it on WhatsApp. You will see the delivery fee before you send, and we confirm your order and delivery time by message.

How do I find out the price of a dish?

Tap Inquire on WhatsApp under any dish and we will reply with the price. Prices are also confirmed before we accept your order.

Can I book a table for a group?

Yes. Use the reservation form for up to 20 guests, or message us on WhatsApp for larger groups and celebrations.

Is there Wi-Fi and parking?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is available for guests, and there is parking for visitors who come by car.

Do you have food for children?

Yes, our kids mini burger plate comes with fries and orange slices, and most pasta, pizza and snack dishes are easy for little ones to share.

Save a table for your next gathering

Birthdays, business lunches or a quiet coffee for two. Tell us the date, time and number of guests.

Terms & Conditions

Orders

Orders and reservations are sent to us on WhatsApp and are confirmed only when we reply. Prices are confirmed on WhatsApp before an order is accepted and may change without notice. Menu photos are for illustration.

Delivery

Delivery fees depend on your area and are shown before you send your order. We may quote a different fee for distant locations. Please make sure your phone is reachable and your address is accurate.

Reservations

Please arrive within 15 minutes of your booking time. We may release a table after that. Contact us on WhatsApp to change or cancel.

Payment and refunds

Payment is made on delivery or at pickup unless we agree otherwise. If something is wrong with your order, tell us right away so we can put it right.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you order or reserve, you give your name, phone number, and for delivery your address. This information is sent to our WhatsApp number to prepare and deliver your order or hold your table.

How we use it

Only to fulfil your request and to contact you about it. We do not sell your data.

Your rights
