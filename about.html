About the restaurant

A place to eat well and feel at home

Jamia Food Mart & Coffee Lounge is a restaurant, coffee lounge and food mart in Nakuru, open every day from 8AM to 11PM.

We cook the food people crave: spiced biryani and curries, juicy burgers, creamy pasta, pizza and grilled meats, with fresh juices, bubble tea and coffee to match.

Our lounge is made for lingering, with comfortable seating, free Wi-Fi, parking and room for groups, families and quiet moments alike.

The Jamia story

Good food brings people together

We began with a simple belief: a meal tastes better when it is shared, and a restaurant should feel like an extension of your own table.

That belief shapes everything, from recipes made with care to a lounge where you are never rushed. We welcome early-morning coffee drinkers, lunch crowds, families, students and night owls alike.

As Nakuru has grown, so has Jamia. We keep our doors open from morning until 11PM and now deliver the same warm welcome to your home through WhatsApp.

Cookies Policy

We use a small number of cookies and similar storage to keep our website working and, only if you accept, to understand how it is used.

What we store

Essential storage remembers your cookie choice and the items in your order basket on your device. Analytics cookies (optional) tell us which pages are visited so we can improve the site. They load only after you press Accept.

Your choice

You can decline analytics, or clear your browser storage at any time. Declining does not affect ordering or reservations.

Terms & Conditions

Orders

Orders and reservations are sent to us on WhatsApp and are confirmed only when we reply. Prices are confirmed on WhatsApp before an order is accepted and may change without notice. Menu photos are for illustration.

Delivery

Delivery fees depend on your area and are shown before you send your order. We may quote a different fee for distant locations. Please make sure your phone is reachable and your address is accurate.

Reservations

Please arrive within 15 minutes of your booking time. We may release a table after that. Contact us on WhatsApp to change or cancel.

Payment and refunds

Payment is made on delivery or at pickup unless we agree otherwise. If something is wrong with your order, tell us right away so we can put it right.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and handle personal data in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

What we collect

When you order or reserve, you give your name, phone number, and for delivery your address. This information is sent to our WhatsApp number to prepare and deliver your order or hold your table.

How we use it

Only to fulfil your request and to contact you about it. We do not sell your data.

Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information by messaging us on WhatsApp at 0710 214 058.
